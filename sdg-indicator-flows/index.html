SDG indicator flows

All 251 indicator listings in the global indicator framework (after the 2026 refinement), traced from their primary sustainability dimension through a topical theme and their goal to the indicator itself. Hover any band, node or indicator for detail; click to pin a selection.

Indicator text and UNSD codes from the UN global indicator framework (A/RES/71/313 annex, with refinements through E/CN.3/2026/12). The dimension and theme columns are an added classification by what each indicator measures, not part of the UN framework. Themes are assigned independently of dimensions, so a theme such as disaster risk or aid and investment flows can draw on more than one dimension. Indicators repeated under several targets appear once per listing, so the 251 listings cover 235 unique UNSD codes. Download the classified workbook (.xlsx).
